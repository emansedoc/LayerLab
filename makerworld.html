Which models can we print?

Every MakerWorld model has a licence, shown on its page. We can print and sell:

  • Models licensed CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or CC BY-ND (most of our picks above)
  • Models you designed yourself, or have a commercial licence for

Many models use MakerWorld's Standard Digital File License (personal use only). Send the link anyway: we'll check it and, if we can't print it, suggest a similar design we can.
