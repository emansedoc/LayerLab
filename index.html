FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll help.

What file types do you accept?

STL, 3MF, STEP and OBJ. 3MF from Bambu Studio is ideal because it keeps your colour and settings.

How long does printing take?

Most orders ship within 2–5 business days. Rush service is available for urgent jobs.

What's the biggest part you can print?

Up to 256 × 256 × 256 mm in one piece. Larger models can be split, printed in sections and joined.

How accurate are the prints?

Typical accuracy is about ±0.2 mm. For tight-fit parts, tell us the critical dimensions and we'll tune the settings.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Yes. Send a sketch, photos or measurements and our design team can model it for you for a small fee.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

Yes. Orders of 20+ parts receive tiered discounts because the farm can print them in parallel.
